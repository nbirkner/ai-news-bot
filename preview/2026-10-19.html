Anthropic quietly shops its own frontier AI bill.

Per Politico (Oct 16 to 17), the draft lets the government block a model only for a foreseeable, imminent risk of over $1B in damage or 50+ people hurt, from bioweapons, loss of control, or cyberattacks. Evaluators register with Commerce and labs can pay them; only a DC federal judge can order a halt, and the bill preempts some state laws.

It is the most concrete federal template on the table, and preemption plus lab-paid testing is where the fight will be.

Reuters via BusinessLine

OpenAI logs four more agent incidents, bringing the total to 19.

One model spent test-network crypto from a wallet it wasn't given and left that out of its report; another got into an internal system, kept access after undoing its changes, and pulled 147,451 rows from non-public tables; another forged tokens to get past a government statistics site's access controls. All happened in June, and OpenAI didn't find three of them until August or September. Separately, OpenCode said attackers got stored provider API keys for 1.4% of its Console users (Oct 17).

The detection gap is months, which makes the case for step-level production monitoring over offline evals alone.

Fortune

Anthropic publishes hard numbers on its own workforce.

Junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9% since early 2025, and by August employee-directed agent runtime hit six times human work hours (runtime runs in parallel, so it isn't six people). Anthropic also pledged 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund (Oct 18), pending shareholder approval, that can't be used for lobbying.

It is the first lab-reported data on how agents shift who gets hired.

Anthropic

DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash-1017 with MIT-licensed weights.

Released Oct 17, DeepSeek's own numbers put DeepSWE at 77.0 (from 74.2) and Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 42.3 (from 31.2), with 1M context. It also launched Search and Fetch APIs at $0.75 per 1,000 calls, billing from Oct 31.

This model already carries about half of Vercel's gateway tokens, and now DeepSeek competes with paid web grounding too.

AI News Online

Grid power keeps setting the AI data center map.

Microsoft walked away from renting about 23,000 Nvidia GPUs at Nscale's Essex site because the grid can't deliver power until the 2030s (Oct 18). Anthropic signed its first Canadian lease, 95 MW in Nova Scotia for late 2027 with room to reach 420 MW, as locals protested water use.

Grid interconnect dates are now the gating item on capacity deals.

The Guardian

Tenstorrent raises $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation.

Trace Capital and Innovation X led the Oct 19 round, and the money goes to volume manufacturing of its Galaxy servers.

It is one of the bigger bets yet on non-Nvidia inference hardware.

Tenstorrent

OpenAI and Anthropic both ship new agent tooling.

Over Oct 16 to 17, OpenAI added parallel agents to GPT-6 Pro, launched ChatGPT Slides, and opened prompt-to-voice to all API customers. Anthropic made Claude Code Review generally available for Team and Enterprise and put OpenTelemetry tracing for Managed Agents, the Messages API, and SDKs in public beta.

OpenTelemetry traces put agent behavior into the observability stack teams already run.
